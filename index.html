Who is writing this.

I'm not a coach and I'm not a guru. I spent years in special forces, where discipline was never a personality trait. It was the line between the men who made it and the men who didn't.

Then I came home, took off the uniform, and lost the thread for a while, the way a lot of us do. Right now I'm 28, two semesters from a civil engineering degree, living back under my parents' roof, rebuilding a life from the studs up.

I write these books from inside the grind, not from some finished mountaintop. Everything in them is what I use on myself, on the days it's hard and the days I don't feel like it. If it works on me, it might work on you.
